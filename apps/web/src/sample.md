# Welcome to Kalkül

A **Markdown editor that calculates.** Just write – every line that looks like a calculation gets its result on the right. *Click a result to copy it.*

## Apartment

rent = 1250 €
utilities = 280 €
power = 65 €
monthly = rent + utilities + power
Per year: monthly * 12

## Project quote

rate = 95 €/h
hours = 32 h/week * 6 weeks
net = hours * rate
Discount: net - 5 %
gross = prev + 19 %

The quote comes to `= gross` – that is `= hours in days` of pure working time.

> **Tip:** Text that isn't a calculation is simply ignored – for example "Meeting 3 - 4 pm". Hover over a variable to see its value.
